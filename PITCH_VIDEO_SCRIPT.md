# HawQ pitch video script

Target length: about 1 minute 40 seconds. The companion deck is `PITCH_SLIDES.html`.

## Slide 1 — HawQ

Hi, I’m the solo builder behind HawQ, building from Lagos, Nigeria. HawQ brings crypto market research into one workspace: DeFi and chain analytics, prediction markets, live spot and perpetual data, and quantitative research.

## Slide 2 — The problem

Crypto context is fragmented. People often move between analytics dashboards, market pages, and prediction platforms to understand what is happening. HawQ puts those views together so users can explore the broader context before forming a market view.

## Slide 3 — Quant research

In Quant Lab, users can compare EMA trend-following and RSI mean-reversion strategies against historical Hyperliquid data. They can account for fees and slippage, include funding data when available, and review risk metrics and a chronological holdout period. These are simulations, not promises of future performance.

## Slide 4 — Research loop

Users choose a market, adjust the assumptions, inspect the results, and can then track a paper position using their own entry price. The position is compared with live market prices, but HawQ does not submit exchange orders or move funds.

## Slide 5 — Why me, why now

I built HawQ solo, across the product interface, API, data ingestion, and research tools. I’m building it because useful crypto information is spread across too many places. My next step is to improve the research experience through feedback and stronger data coverage, while keeping simulated trading clearly separate from real execution.

Thanks for taking a look at HawQ.
