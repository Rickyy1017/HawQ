Product · 03
Quant research

From market history
to a testable strategy.

Quant Lab compares two transparent models—EMA trend following and RSI mean reversion—against historical Hyperliquid data.

EMATrend-following model
RSIMean-reversion model
CostsFees, slippage & available funding
HoldoutChronological out-of-sample window
Results are historical simulations, not predictions. The model does not simulate order-book depth, liquidation, queue position, or market impact.

Workflow · 04
A practical research loop

Inspect. Compare. Simulate.

01 · ChooseSelect a market

Pick an asset and historical candle interval.

02 · ConfigureSet your assumptions

Adjust model periods, capital, fees, and slippage.

03 · EvaluateReview the evidence

Compare returns, drawdown, Sharpe, trades, funding, and holdout results.

04 · Paper tradeTrack a position

Enter your own price and monitor a simulated position against live prices.

Paper positions are stored in this browser. HawQ does not send exchange orders or move funds.
